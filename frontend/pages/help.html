How it works

Help & safe handover guide

Follow this process to report items, review suggestions, and return belongings safely.

1

Report an item

Choose Report Lost for an item you own, or Report Found when you have found an item. Found reports require a photo; a lost report photo is optional.

2

Add private details

For a lost report, provide the interior, unique marks, and contents. These private details are kept out of normal item and match views.

3

Review a possible match

The system compares color, location, and keywords to rank found-item candidates. A similarity score is a suggestion, not proof of ownership.

4

Create a claim

The owner of the lost report selects Claim this item. This creates a pending claim; it does not automatically approve a handover.

5

Staff reviews saved details

You do not answer the same questions again. An authorised staff member compares the private details saved with your lost report against the found report. The finder cannot see those details.

6

Complete the handover

If staff approves the claim, arrange the handover through the campus process. Staff records completion, and then both reports become resolved.

Notifications

Use the Notifications page to see potential matches, report updates, and claim changes. Settings lets you choose in-app alerts, email alerts, and the event types you want to receive.

Why can’t I claim an item?

You must be signed in as the owner of an active lost report with complete private details. The found report must be active and belong to a different finder. A claim begins staff review; it does not automatically approve a handover.

Stay safe

Do not put phone numbers, bank details, Aadhaar, PAN, card numbers, or other sensitive information in reports. Arrange handovers in a safe campus location.
